Braids are a well-known subject of study in algebraic topology. My project generalizes our description of braids for cyclotomic braids, a recent abstraction which emerges in quantum algebra. This was written under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Zsuzsanna Dancso, for SCDL3991 (Science Dalyell Individual Research Project), a semester long research unit.
